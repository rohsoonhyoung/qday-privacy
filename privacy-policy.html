Q-Day Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026
Effective date: May 22, 2026

Developer: Unibox Labs | App: Q-Day

Unibox Labs provides Q-Day as a free mood journal and check-in app. This Privacy Policy explains what information Q-Day collects or processes, how it is used, when it may be shared, and what choices users have.

Important privacy design: daily prompt answers, diary text, diary photos, private emotion notes, context tags, reminder settings, theme settings, and most journal history are stored on your device and are not uploaded to Q-Day servers unless you explicitly use a public or online feature described below.

1. Information We Collect or Process

1.1 App operation and anonymous IDs

  • Anonymous Firebase Authentication ID used to operate anonymous emotion statistics and prevent duplicate check-ins.
  • Anonymous Appwrite session/user ID used for public messages, nickname profiles, and leaderboard features.
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and basic diagnostic information.

1.2 Emotion check-ins and statistics

  • Main daily emotion selection, check-in date, and related timestamp may be stored with an anonymous identifier in Firebase Firestore.
  • Aggregated emotion counts are used to show anonymous community statistics.
  • Additional private emotion records, comments, context tags, diary text, prompt answers, and diary photos are stored locally on your device.

1.3 Optional public and online features

  • If you post a public one-line message, Q-Day stores the message content, anonymous Appwrite user ID, date, creation time, daily posting count, and moderation/block status. Public messages may be visible to other users.
  • If you set a nickname, Q-Day stores the nickname, anonymous Appwrite user ID, and creation time. Nicknames may be shown in public features such as rankings.
  • If you submit an Emotion 2048 leaderboard score, Q-Day stores the nickname, anonymous Appwrite user ID, score, highest tile/emotion value, and timestamps. Leaderboard entries may be visible to other users.

1.4 Analytics, ads, and consent

  • Q-Day uses Firebase Analytics to understand aggregated app usage, screen views, and feature events.
  • Q-Day uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may process advertising identifiers, device information, IP-derived approximate location, ad interaction data, and consent signals where available.
  • Where legally required, Q-Day uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent before ad requests are made. If consent is not available, ads may be limited or not shown.

1.5 Device-only information

  • Prompt answers, diary text, diary photos selected by the user, private emotion comments, custom context tags, local statistics, mini-game progress, reminder time, notification preference, language setting, and theme setting are stored on the device.
  • Backup/export files are created only when you use the backup or export feature. If you choose to include photos, the backup can contain diary photos and should be kept private by you.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide mood check-ins, journaling, calendar, analysis, backup/export, and mini-game features.
  • Show anonymous community emotion statistics and prevent duplicate daily main check-ins.
  • Operate optional public message, nickname, and leaderboard features.
  • Improve app reliability, usability, and performance through aggregated analytics.
  • Show and measure ads, manage ad consent, and help prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Respond to user requests, support inquiries, legal obligations, and security issues.

3. Third-Party Services

Q-Day uses the following service providers. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase Authentication, Firebase Firestore, and Firebase Analytics: Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform: Google AdMob policies and restrictions and Google Privacy Policy
  • Appwrite backend services operated for Q-Day public messages, profiles, and leaderboards.
  • Google Play services and Android platform services required for app distribution, updates, permissions, and billing-free app delivery.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share or disclose information only in these cases:

  • With service providers listed above to operate the app.
  • When you choose to use public features, such as public messages, nicknames, and leaderboard entries, which may be visible to other users.
  • To comply with applicable law, legal process, or lawful government requests.
  • To protect users, prevent abuse, troubleshoot security issues, or enforce service rules.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of the service, with appropriate notice where required.

5. Permissions and Device Access

  • Internet and network state: used for Firebase, Appwrite, AdMob, analytics, and public online features.
  • Notifications: requested only if you choose to enable daily reminders on supported Android versions.
  • Vibration: used for optional mini-game feedback.
  • Photos selected by you: when you add diary photos, Q-Day copies only selected files into app storage. Photos are not uploaded to Q-Day servers.

Q-Day does not request precise location, contacts, microphone, SMS, call log, or camera permissions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local journal data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Local diary photos remain on your device until deleted in the app, included in a backup you create, cleared with app data, or removed by uninstalling the app.
  • Anonymous emotion statistics and related main check-in records may be retained to provide service statistics. Aggregated statistics may remain after deletion because they are designed not to identify users directly.
  • Public messages, nicknames, message counts, and leaderboard entries are retained while the related public feature is operated, unless deleted or blocked through moderation or a deletion request.
  • Analytics and advertising data are retained by Google according to Google's policies and your device/account settings.

You can delete local data from app settings, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of server-side records that may be linked to your anonymous IDs, contact us at rhsoh1@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to locate the relevant anonymous records.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can choose not to use optional public features such as public messages, nicknames, and leaderboards.
  • You can enable or disable reminders in app settings and Android system settings.
  • You can manage ad personalization and advertising ID settings in Android or Google settings.
  • Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing of your information.

8. International Processing

Q-Day is available globally. Service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence, including Korea, the United States, or other locations where Google, Appwrite, or their infrastructure providers operate.

9. Children

Q-Day is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as HTTPS transport, Firebase security rules, access controls, and local device storage. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If changes are material, we may provide additional notice in the app where appropriate.

1. 수집 또는 처리하는 정보

1.1 앱 운영 및 익명 ID

  • 익명 감정 통계 운영 및 중복 기록 방지를 위한 Firebase 익명 인증 ID
  • 공개 메시지, 닉네임 프로필, 랭킹 기능을 위한 Appwrite 익명 세션/사용자 ID
  • 기기 모델, 운영체제 버전, 앱 버전, 언어, 기본 진단 정보 등 기기 및 앱 정보

1.2 감정 기록 및 통계

  • 대표 일일 감정 선택, 기록 날짜, 관련 시간이 익명 식별자와 함께 Firebase Firestore에 저장될 수 있습니다.
  • 집계된 감정 수치는 익명 커뮤니티 통계를 표시하는 데 사용됩니다.
  • 추가 감정 기록, 코멘트, 상황 태그, 일기 본문, 질문 답변, 일기 사진은 사용자 기기에 로컬로 저장됩니다.

1.3 선택적 공개 및 온라인 기능

  • 공개 한줄 메시지를 작성하면 메시지 내용, 익명 Appwrite 사용자 ID, 날짜, 작성 시간, 일일 작성 횟수, 차단/운영 상태가 저장됩니다. 공개 메시지는 다른 사용자에게 보일 수 있습니다.
  • 닉네임을 설정하면 닉네임, 익명 Appwrite 사용자 ID, 생성 시간이 저장됩니다. 닉네임은 랭킹 등 공개 기능에 표시될 수 있습니다.
  • 감정 2048 리더보드 점수를 제출하면 닉네임, 익명 Appwrite 사용자 ID, 점수, 최고 타일/감정 값, 시간이 저장됩니다. 리더보드 기록은 다른 사용자에게 보일 수 있습니다.

1.4 분석, 광고 및 동의

  • Q-Day는 Firebase Analytics를 사용하여 집계된 앱 사용, 화면 조회, 기능 이벤트를 파악합니다.
  • Q-Day는 Google AdMob을 사용하여 광고를 표시합니다. Google은 광고 식별자, 기기 정보, IP 기반 대략적 위치, 광고 상호작용 데이터, 동의 신호를 처리할 수 있습니다.
  • 법적으로 필요한 경우 Q-Day는 Google User Messaging Platform을 통해 광고 요청 전에 동의를 요청합니다. 동의 상태에 따라 광고가 제한되거나 표시되지 않을 수 있습니다.

  • 질문 답변, 일기 본문, 사용자가 선택한 일기 사진, 개인 감정 코멘트, 사용자 지정 상황 태그, 로컬 통계, 미니게임 진행 상태, 알림 시간, 알림 설정, 언어 설정, 테마 설정은 기기에 저장됩니다.
  • 백업/내보내기 파일은 사용자가 해당 기능을 사용할 때만 생성됩니다. 사진 포함 백업을 선택하면 일기 사진이 포함될 수 있으므로 사용자가 안전하게 보관해야 합니다.
